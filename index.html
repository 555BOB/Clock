About

Quarterbell

Feel time passing, without watching the clock

If you're hyperfocused, an hour can vanish in what feels like ten minutes. If you're waiting, ten minutes can feel like an hour. For many people with ADHD, and for anyone who's ever looked up and wondered where the afternoon went, time isn't something you naturally feel. It's something you keep losing.

Quarterbell gives time back its sound. Like the clock towers that once kept whole towns on schedule, it gently chimes on the hour and every quarter hour, so you always have a sense of where you are in the day without breaking your focus to check.

No more surfacing from deep work to find you've missed a meeting. With Chime earlier, the bell rings up to two minutes before the hour, a gentle heads-up that gives you time to wrap up, stand up and switch gears instead of scrambling.

You decide when it rings. Choose your hours and days, pick a sound for each chime, from a soft bell to a grandfather clock, and set the face and background to match your space. Then put it in full screen, set it down, and let it keep time for you.

Calm, beautiful and simple, Quarterbell doesn't nag, buzz or flood you with notifications. It just quietly helps you stay in the moment and on time.
