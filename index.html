Add Chime Clock to your Home Screen

It opens like an app — full screen, no address bar.

  1. 1Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.Can’t see it? Tap the ⋯ button first, then Share.
  2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen Not there? Tap Edit Actions at the bottom of the list to add it.
  3. 3Check Open as Web App is on, if you see it, then tap Add.

Apple only allows adding to the Home Screen from Safari. Copy the link, open Safari, paste it in, then tap Add to Home Screen again.
